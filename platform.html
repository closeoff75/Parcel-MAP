CONTINUOUS CADASTRAL PIPELINE

Automated Real-Time Ingestion to Sub-Centimeter GIS

How raw municipal recorder deeds and GIS surveyor shapefiles are ingested, geometrically cleansed, and delivered to your application in under 20 milliseconds.

1

Continuous County Ingestion & Optical Metes-and-Bounds Parsing

Our distributed scraping workers continuously monitor County Clerk recorder feeds, pulling recorded warranty deeds, parcel split surveys, and legal metes-and-bounds descriptions.

2

Automated Topological Cleansing & Edge Snapping

Raw municipal polygons frequently feature slivers, overlapping easements, and outdated datum projections. Our spatial engine enforces planar topology, snapping common boundaries with sub-centimeter tolerances.

3

Multi-Scale H3 Hexagonal & Dynamic MVT Vector Tiling

Pre-compiled spatial indices (Uber H3 & Quadtree) allow instant bounding-box lookups at zoom levels 0 through 22, pre-baked with tax assessment records and zoning classifications.

4

Global Edge Delivery & WebSocket Cadastre Stream

Cached across 280+ global edge Points-of-Presence (PoPs). Client map viewers receive lightweight binary protobuf tiles with zero-flash rendering.
