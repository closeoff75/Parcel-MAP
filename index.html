HD PARCEL ANALYSIS • SATELLITE
END-TO-END GEOTECH WORKFLOW

Drone Imagery → Verified Parcel Map

From high-resolution UAV aerial data to authoritative GIS parcels using road-based spatial reasoning and human-in-the-loop verification.

1

CAPTURE

Drone captures high-resolution UAV orthomosaics with sub-3cm GSD resolution.
2

DETECT

AI classifies roads, building footprints, stone walls, wire fences, and field edges.
3

REASON

Road-based spatial reasoning uses the road network as reference to infer preliminary parcels.
4

GENERATE

GIS engine generates polygons with temporary parcel IDs (PM-0001...) and area math.
5

VALIDATE

Quality control identifies topology gaps, boundary overlaps, and sliver artifacts.
6

VERIFY

Surveyors verify, edit vertices, split/merge boundaries, and sign off the verified map.
